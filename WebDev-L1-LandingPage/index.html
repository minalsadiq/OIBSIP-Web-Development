Our strict 3-step method

How we craft our coffee

Every Tuesday and Friday at our Faisalabad roastery, we follow an exact, deliberate process to ensure absolute freshness in every bag.

  1. 1

    Ethical sourcing from 6 partner farms

    We source green coffee beans imported directly from verified farms across Ethiopia, Sumatra, and Colombia, ensuring fair trade practices and complete origin traceability.

  2. 2

    Small-batch drum roasting

    We roast precisely 12 kilos at a time in our vintage drum roaster, relying on visual cues, aroma, and sound rather than automated computer profiles.

  3. 3

    Ten-day resting window

    Every freshly roasted batch is allowed to rest and degas for ten full days before packaging, so it achieves peak flavor profiles before reaching your cup.
